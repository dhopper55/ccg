The Prize

1999 Fender Standard Telecaster.

Midnight Blue Metallic · Made in Mexico

Very clean 1999 Fender Standard Telecaster, Made in Mexico, in the beautiful Midnight Blue Metallic finish. One of the nicer late-90s MIM Teles, with the classic alder body, maple neck, and unmistakable Tele twang and snap.

The Midnight Blue Metallic finish looks fantastic in person and shifts between deep blue and purple tones depending on the lighting — a standout color that sets it apart from the usual black and sunburst Teles.

Freshly cleaned, restrung, and fully set up by Coal Creek Guitars. Plays comfortably with good action and classic Fender feel. Electronics tested and working properly. Overall condition is very good for a 1999 guitar, with normal cosmetic wear from age and use.

  • Freshly cleaned, restrung & set up
