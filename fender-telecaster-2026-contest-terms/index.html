Official Rules

Coal Creek Guitars "Win a Fender Telecaster" Giveaway

Effective August 23, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED BY LAW.

1. Sponsor

This giveaway ("Giveaway") is sponsored by Coal Creek Guitars, located at 4109 S Sherman St, Suite B, Englewood, CO 80113 ("Sponsor").

2. Eligibility

The Giveaway is open to legal residents of the United States who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of majority in their state of residence) at the time of entry.

Only legal residents of the State of Colorado, age 18 or older, are eligible to be awarded and claim the prize. Entrants who do not reside in Colorado may enter but are not eligible to win; if such an entrant is randomly selected, they will be disqualified and an alternate entrant will be selected as described in Section 9 below.

Employees of Coal Creek Guitars and their immediate family members (spouse, parents, children, siblings) or household members are not eligible to participate.

3. Entry Period

The Giveaway begins on August 23, 2026 at 12:00 AM MST and ends on October 31, 2026 at 12:00 PM (Noon) MST ("Entry Period"). All entries must be received within the Entry Period to be eligible.

4. How to Enter

Entrants may enter via the entry form hosted at coalcreekguitars.com (powered by SweepWidget). Before accessing the entry form, entrants will be asked to self-certify that they are a legal resident of Colorado; this self-certification is not independently verified at the time of entry and does not replace the residency verification process described in Section 8. No purchase is necessary. To enter, complete the following actions, each of which earns one (1) entry:

  1. Submit your full name and email address, and agree to join the Coal Creek Guitars email list. This is required to participate.
  2. Follow Coal Creek Guitars on Instagram (@coalcreekguitars).
  3. Visit the Coal Creek Guitars Facebook Page (facebook.com/profile.php?id=61587059786524).

Bonus entries: After entering, each entrant receives a unique referral link. For each additional person who enters the Giveaway using that referral link, the referring entrant receives one (1) additional entry.

Entry limit: Each entrant may earn a maximum of twenty-five (25) total entries, combining entries earned through the actions above and through referrals, regardless of how that total is reached.

Entrants may enter using only one email address and one identity. Use of multiple email addresses, fake accounts, bots, scripts, or any other automated or fraudulent means to obtain additional entries is prohibited and will result in disqualification.

5. Prize

One (1) winner will receive:

  • One (1) 1999 Fender Standard Telecaster, Midnight Blue Metallic finish (Made in Mexico), freshly cleaned, restrung, and set up
  • One (1) new Fender-branded gig bag

Approximate Retail Value (ARV): $600 USD.

No cash alternative or substitution of the prize is permitted, except at Sponsor's sole discretion, in which case a prize of equal or greater value may be substituted.

Prize pickup: The prize must be picked up in person at Coal Creek Guitars' physical location in Englewood, CO. No shipping or delivery is available; local pickup only.

6. Odds of Winning

Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received during the Entry Period.

7. Winner Selection

One (1) potential winner will be selected in a random drawing from all eligible entries on October 31, 2026 at 8:00 PM MST, conducted via SweepWidget's randomized selection tool.

8. Winner Notification & Verification

The potential winner will be contacted directly by Sponsor via the email and/or phone number provided at entry.

  • The potential winner must respond within 72 hours of being contacted. Failure to respond within this window will result in disqualification.
  • The potential winner must provide a valid Colorado driver's license or Colorado state-issued ID showing a Colorado address, to confirm eligibility under Section 2.
  • Failure to provide valid Colorado residency verification will result in disqualification.

9. Alternate Winners

If a potential winner is disqualified for any reason under Section 8 (non-response, inability to verify Colorado residency, or otherwise fails to meet eligibility requirements), Sponsor will select a new potential winner via random drawing from the remaining eligible entries. This process will repeat, with no limit on the number of redraws, until a winner is confirmed who satisfies all eligibility and verification requirements.

10. Prize Claim

Once residency is verified, Sponsor will coordinate a pickup appointment with the winner. The winner has thirty (30) days from the date they are confirmed as the winner to pick up the prize in person at Coal Creek Guitars in Englewood, CO. If the prize is not picked up within this window, it will be forfeited, and Sponsor reserves the right to award it to an alternate winner or retain it.

11. Publicity

At the time of prize pickup, Sponsor may request to take a photograph of the winner holding the prize, to be used in Sponsor's winner announcement across its social media and marketing channels. Agreeing to be photographed is optional and is not a condition of receiving the prize. If the winner declines, Sponsor may still announce that a winner was selected without using the winner's image, and will not use the winner's full name or likeness without separate permission.

12. Taxes

The winner is solely responsible for any and all federal, state, and local taxes associated with acceptance and use of the prize.
