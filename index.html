Our Story

A Shop Rooted in the Instrument.

Coal Creek Guitars was founded in 2017 by lifelong players and music enthusiasts who wanted to build something more personal than a typical gear dealer.

We source quality used instruments, improve them — from a full setup and deep clean to part replacements and damage repair — and return them to the market. Profit matters, but the chance to learn from a restoration is just as important.

We're located in Englewood, Colorado, just south of Denver along the South Broadway corridor, steps from one of Colorado's most iconic live music venues.

Our Neighborhood

A Street That's Always Been Loud.

The Gothic Theatre — one of Colorado's most storied venues, where Nirvana debuted "Smells Like Teen Spirit" in 1991 — sits just up South Broadway from our shop. Englewood's music history runs deep, and Coal Creek Guitars is proud to be part of it.
