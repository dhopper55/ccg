### How to Enter
Entry is run through SweepWidget, embedded directly on coalcreekguitars.com. Three required actions, each worth 1 entry:

1. **Join our email list** — enter name + email (this is the base entry; required to participate at all)
2. **Follow us on Instagram**
3. **Visit our Facebook Page** (Facebook's own rules don't allow "like/follow" as a required entry action — "visit page" is the compliant equivalent; there's also an optional checkbox inviting them to like the page, just not required)

**Bonus entries — refer a friend:**
Each entrant gets a unique referral link. Every friend who signs up for the email list using that link earns the referrer an additional entry.

**Entry cap:** Maximum of **25 total entries (points) per person**, combining the 3 base actions plus any referral bonuses.

---

### Who Can Win
Entry itself is open — since we're on SweepWidget's lower-tier plan, there's no automatic technical block on who can sign up. **However, only Colorado residents are eligible to actually claim the prize.**

If the randomly selected winner is not a Colorado resident (or can't be verified as one), they are disqualified and passed over — we redraw a new name.

---

### Winner Selection & Verification Process

1. **Winner is randomly drawn** on October 31 at 8:00 PM MST via SweepWidget.
2. **We reach out directly** to the winner (email/phone).
3. **Winner has 72 hours to respond.** No response within 72 hours = forfeited, move to next step.
4. **Residency verification:** winner must provide a Colorado driver's license or state-issued ID showing a Colorado address.
5. **If the winner doesn't respond in time, or can't verify Colorado residency:** they're disqualified, and we redraw a new name.
   - **This repeats with no set limit** — we keep redrawing until a verified Colorado resident responds and confirms. (Note: this means the process could theoretically take a while if you hit a run of non-CO or unresponsive entrants — worth knowing that going in, though realistically it should resolve within a handful of draws given your CO-focused audience.)
6. **Once residency is confirmed,** we coordinate a pickup time directly with the winner.

### Prize Pickup
- **Local pickup only — Englewood, CO.** No shipping, no exceptions.
- **Winner has 30 days from being confirmed as the winner to physically pick up the guitar**, or the prize is forfeited.
- **At pickup, we'll ask to take a photo of the winner holding the guitar for our winner announcement.** This is **optional but preferred** — not a condition of receiving the prize. If the winner declines, they still get the guitar; we'll just announce the win without a photo (or with their permission to share just their first name).
