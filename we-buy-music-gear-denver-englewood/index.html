How You Get Paid.

We don't keep a lot of cash on site, but we have multiple ways to pay you — and we'll work with your preference whenever possible.

Option 01
Cash & Company Check

We keep cash on hand for smaller purchases. For larger transactions, we pay by official company check — easily deposited at your bank or through mobile banking. If you're expecting a larger deal, just mention it when you reach out so we can plan accordingly.

Option 02
Venmo, Zelle, Cash App & PayPal

Digital payment works for many transactions and is often the quickest way to get paid. Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, and PayPal are all options depending on the amount and your preference. If you have a preferred method, let us know — we'll do our best to make it work.

Option 03 · Most Value
Store Credit

If you're a player who's going to buy more gear eventually, store credit is almost always the best deal in the room. When payment stays with us, we can typically offer more in store credit than we could in cash. Think of it less as selling and more like trading up.

Store Credit · The Hidden Advantage

Guitar Center's cash offer is Guitar Center's cash offer — take it or leave it. Our store credit works differently. Because that value comes back to us, we have more flexibility in what we can offer you. In many cases, players who chose store credit at Coal Creek Guitars walked away with significantly more purchasing power than the cash offer from a chain store would have given them. If you're already thinking about your next guitar, run the numbers both ways before you decide.

Why We Often Beat Guitar Center.

We hear this regularly: someone visits Guitar Center, gets an offer, and walks away disappointed. Then they find us. Here's why our offers tend to look different.

The Corporate Margin Problem

Guitar Center's buying price has to work within a national pricing structure with fixed margins, regional quotas, and overhead from hundreds of locations. Their offer to you isn't based purely on what your guitar is worth — it's based on what their system allows them to pay and still hit their numbers.

We don't operate that way. We're one shop. Our overhead is lower, our decision-making is faster, and we're not answering to a regional buying manager in another state. When we research your gear's current market value, that's what we base our offer on — not a formula designed to protect a corporate margin.

We can't promise we'll beat their offer every single time. But in our experience, we beat it more often than not — sometimes by a meaningful amount. The only way to find out is to ask us both.

Consignment: Let's Talk.

We don't have a formal, standardized consignment program in place yet — but we're not saying no. For the right piece of gear, we're absolutely willing to have a conversation.

Better Than Most Consignment Shops

The standard consignment deal at most shops takes 30–40% off the top. For a $1,200 guitar, that's $360–$480 out of your pocket before you see a dime. If your gear sits for months, that cut gets harder to justify.

We're not locked into a standard percentage. For the right instrument — something with demand, at a realistic price, that fits what we carry — we're willing to negotiate a split that's better than what most consignment shops offer. We have to evaluate it case by case, but we'd rather work with you than lose you to a shop that takes 40%.

Reach out, tell us what you have, and we'll have an honest conversation about whether it makes sense for both of us.

How to Get Started.

The process is simple. You don't need to haul your gear across town just to find out what it's worth. Start with a message.

01
Tell Us What You Have

Reach out by email, text, or phone. The more detail the better: brand, model, year, serial number if you have it, and a general sense of condition. Mention any modifications, case, original hardware, or accessories included. You can also submit through our contact page or free valuation form.

02
Send Photos

Pictures are the single most important thing for an accurate initial estimate. Don't overthink it — shoot the front, back, headstock, and any wear or damage. If there's something specific about the condition, get a close-up. Good photos let us give you a real number, not a vague range.

03
Get Your Offer

We'll come back with a fair, research-based valuation. No pressure, no obligation. If the numbers work for you, we'll arrange a time to come by or coordinate pickup. If they don't, we'll tell you why and you're free to explore other options — no hard feelings, ever.
