Got a Collection? We Come to You.

This is where we do our best work. Estates. Studio closures. A band breaking up. Someone finally ready to deal with a garage full of gear that isn't even theirs to begin with — like a late parent's workshop. If you've got more than a piece or two, we'll come to you anywhere in the Denver metro and take everything, not just the easy pieces.

01
Tell Us What You've Got

Call, text, or email — whose gear it was, roughly how much there is, and the general condition. No forms, no inventory spreadsheet required. Just a piece or two? Send photos and we can usually give you a ballpark right away.

02
We Come to You

For a real collection — the good ones — we'll drive out anywhere in the Denver metro to see it in person. The bigger or farther the lot, the more it helps to talk and see a few photos first so the trip is worth everyone's time.

03
Cash Offer, Same Visit

Once we've seen it, you'll usually have a detailed estimate by web link within a few hours. Agree on a number and we do the rest — we haul it all out ourselves, including the broken stuff and the parts bins.

True Story

One of the best buys we ever made looked, at first glance, like a pile of trash. A woman reached out about her late father's workshop — years of amp parts, half-finished projects, gear in pieces. It didn't look like much in the photos. We went and looked anyway, and ended up paying a lot more than she expected. Once you know what you're looking at, that "junk pile" is often worth real money. Your gear doesn't need to be organized, cleaned up, or even working for us to be interested.

Coal Creek Guitars is based in Englewood, Colorado, on the South Broadway corridor just south of Denver — by appointment only, so give us a call or send a message before stopping by. For house calls, we serve the entire Denver metro: Englewood, Denver, Littleton, Centennial, Aurora, Greenwood Village, Highlands Ranch, and beyond. If you've got a full collection to sell, distance is rarely the deciding factor — the size of the lot is, and we'll gladly go farther for the right one.

How You Get Paid.

For a single item, it's simple. For a full collection, we bring the payment to you — cash or check, right there when we haul it away.

Option 01
Cash & Company Check

For house calls and larger collections, we come prepared to pay cash or by official company check on the spot — no waiting, no follow-up trip. For smaller in-shop sales, it works the same way.

Option 02
Venmo, Zelle, Cash App & PayPal

Digital payment works for many transactions and is often the quickest way to get paid. Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, and PayPal are all options depending on the amount and your preference. If you have a preferred method, let us know — we'll do our best to make it work.

Option 03 · Most Value
Store Credit

If you're a player who's going to buy more gear eventually, store credit is almost always the best deal in the room. When payment stays with us, we can typically offer more in store credit than we could in cash. Think of it less as selling and more like trading up.

Store Credit · The Hidden Advantage

If you're already thinking about your next guitar, store credit is usually the best deal in the room. Because that value stays with us instead of leaving as cash, we can typically offer more in credit than we could in a cash payout. If you're on the fence, run the numbers both ways before you decide.

Or Consign It: You Keep 80%.

If you'd rather not sell outright today, we run a formal consignment program — not a case-by-case handshake. The standard deal at most shops takes 30–40% off the top; ours is an 80/20 split in your favor, with zero fees deducted from your share on a local sale.

Denver's Best-Looking Consignment Listings

Every consigned item gets studio-quality photography, a detailed write-up, and placement in our showroom, our online shop, and — with your approval — Reverb.com. It's marketed exactly the way we'd market something we own outright.

You and we agree on a listing price and pricing range together up front, you're paid within 14 days of a sale via Venmo, Zelle, or bank transfer, and if it's ever damaged, lost, or stolen while it's with us, we pay you 80% of its current listed price.
