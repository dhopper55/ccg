Report sections

Every section, explained.

01
Identity

Exact model, year, factory, and run. We decode your serial number and identify the specific variant — Custom Shop vs USA, production run, and any factory options matter enormously to price.

02
Photo Analysis

Annotated plates of your submitted photos — body, headstock, neck, hardware, electronics cavity — with condition notes for each area.

03
Full Specifications

Complete spec sheet: body wood, top, neck profile, fretboard, scale length, hardware grade, finish, electronics, and everything that ships with the instrument.

04
Market Comps

Actual sold listings from Reverb and eBay, plus current dealer asks. You see the real comps we used, not just a number with no paper trail.

05
Four-Channel Valuation

New/street retail, Reverb/eBay sold, private & local sale, and a dealer/shop offer range — so you know exactly what to expect in every scenario.

06
What Helps & Hurts

The specific factors on your instrument that push the value up or down — case, COA, top figure, pickup type, weight, repairs — with dollar-level context for each.

07
What to Verify

A checklist of anything you should confirm before listing or accepting an offer — authentication points, hardware details, and condition factors we flagged from your photos.

08
Copy-Ready Listing

A title and full description optimized for Reverb, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, and Craigslist — with platform-specific guidance for each. Fill the blanks and post.
