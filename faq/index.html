Visiting Us

Where are you located?

Coal Creek Guitars is located in Englewood, Colorado. We now have a dedicated showroom where players can check out guitars, amps, and gear in person. While we do not have regular public business hours quite yet, we plan to officially open with scheduled store hours in Summer 2026.

In the meantime, we are happy to meet by appointment. If you see something you are interested in, just contact us and we can arrange a time for you to come by, plug in, and spend some time with the gear. We also keep a nice selection of amps and effects available so players can dial in the tone and feel they are looking for.

Curious about the neighborhood? Learn more about Englewood, the historic Broadway corridor, and the Gothic Theatre — the area that Coal Creek Guitars calls home.

What are your hours?

At the moment, Coal Creek Guitars is operating by appointment only. We are preparing for regular showroom hours in Summer 2026, but for now we prefer to schedule individual visits so we can give each customer a more personal experience.

If there is a guitar or piece of gear you would like to see, simply contact us and we will coordinate a convenient time for you to stop by and try it out.

Are you open to the public & do you require appointments?

Yes — our showroom is open to customers, but visits are currently by appointment only. Since we are still building out the showroom experience ahead of our planned Summer 2026 public hours launch, appointments help us make sure someone is available to assist you and have the gear ready to go when you arrive.

We want customers to feel comfortable taking their time with instruments and equipment, not rushed through a crowded retail environment. If there is something you would like to try, just reach out and we will gladly set up a visit.

Can I play guitars in the showroom?

Absolutely. We encourage players to spend time with the gear and make sure it feels right before buying. Our showroom includes a variety of amps and effects so you can experiment with different sounds, styles, and tones while you play.

Whether you are testing a beginner setup, comparing guitars, or chasing a specific sound, we want you to have a relaxed environment where you can really experience the instrument.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. The owners of Coal Creek Guitars come from a proud military family, and we are incredibly grateful for the service and sacrifice of our veterans and active-duty military members. As a small way of saying thank you, we offer a 12% discount off our lowest price on one eligible item.

That is higher than many major retailers offer, and unlike some national chains, we do not cap the maximum discount amount. We appreciate your service and are glad to offer something meaningful when we can.

Selling Your Gear

What is the Coal Creek Guitar Evaluation Report and how does it work?

The Coal Creek Guitar Evaluation Report is a free, no-obligation assessment that gives you an honest picture of what your guitar or gear is worth in today's used market. Whether you are thinking about selling, trading in, or just curious about the current value of an instrument you own, the report helps set realistic expectations before you bring anything in or make a decision.

To get started, you provide details about your guitar — brand, model, year, condition, and any modifications or accessories included. We research current market data, recent comparable sales, and condition-based pricing to put together a personalized valuation. The report reflects what the gear is actually selling for right now, not just the asking price on used gear sites.

The evaluation is completely free and there is no obligation to sell or trade. It is simply a resource to help you make an informed decision. Submit your guitar for a free evaluation report here.

Do you buy used instruments and gear?

Absolutely — buying used guitars, amps, pedals, and music gear is a big part of what we do at Coal Creek Guitars. We are always interested in quality used equipment and enjoy giving great gear a second life.

That said, we do not purchase every item that comes through the door. All gear must be evaluated in person before we can determine whether it is something we are interested in purchasing. We strongly recommend contacting us first with pictures and basic information about the gear so we can review it before you make the trip.

Please note that bringing gear to Coal Creek Guitars does not guarantee we will make an offer or purchase the item. Interest depends on condition, market demand, current inventory, and overall fit for the shop.

What kinds of music gear do you buy?

We buy a wide variety of music gear, with guitars, pedals, and effects equipment generally being our highest priority. We also consider amplifiers, recording gear, accessories, and other music-related equipment depending on the item, condition, and current shop needs.

Smaller and easier-to-transport gear tends to be a better fit for us than very large or oversized items, but we are always happy to take a look. In addition to instruments and amps, we also purchase things like unopened string packs, used straps, cables, cases, stands, pedals, and other useful accessories musicians regularly need.

If you are unsure whether your gear is something we would be interested in, feel free to contact us with pictures and details before bringing it in.

If we agree on a price for you to buy my gear, do you pay in cash?

We do not keep cash on-site at the store. Most purchases are paid by official company check, which can be deposited directly through your bank or mobile banking app.

Depending on the amount and situation, we may also be able to pay electronically through services such as Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or PayPal. If you have a preferred payment method, feel free to ask and we will do our best to accommodate it when possible.

We also offer store credit as an option, which can be a great way to maximize value if you are planning to pick up other gear from Coal Creek Guitars.

Can I trade in my gear and get credit towards another purchase?

Absolutely. We are always open to trade-ins and store credit deals at Coal Creek Guitars. If you have gear you are no longer using, we are happy to take a look and see if we can work something out.

Please refer to the FAQ above regarding the types of gear we typically buy and accept. If we come to an agreement on the trade-in value of your equipment, we can apply that amount as store credit toward another guitar, amp, pedal, accessory, or other item in the shop.

Do you do consignment?

Currently, Coal Creek Guitars does not officially offer a formal consignment program. However, for the right piece of gear, we are always willing to have a conversation and see if something makes sense for both parties.

We are still working on formalizing our long-term consignment policies and procedures, but in the meantime, unofficial consignment arrangements may be possible on a case-by-case basis depending on the item, value, demand, and overall fit for the shop. If you have something you think may be a good candidate, feel free to contact us and start the conversation.

Buying Gear

I want to buy something, but don't have the funds. What are my options?

Online checkout is available by credit or debit card and other standard Stripe payment methods, but financing is not offered for online purchases.

For eligible larger purchases made in the showroom, qualified customers may be able to use Affirm or Klarna financing through Stripe, subject to approval. Financing purchases remain eligible for our return policy, but approved financing returns are subject to a 6% return processing/restocking fee because the financing provider fees are not refunded to Coal Creek Guitars.

For certain types of gear, Coal Creek Guitars may also be willing to offer a rent-to-own option. These arrangements are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on the item and situation, and generally require a valid credit card to remain on file.

Do you have gear that is not listed on your website?

Absolutely. We often have a large amount of incoming gear that gets cleaned, tested, photographed, and listed gradually over time. Because of that, not everything we currently have available will always appear on the website right away.

If you are looking for a specific type of guitar, amp, pedal, accessory, or other piece of gear and do not see it listed on the Coal Creek Guitars website, feel free to reach out through our Contact Us page and ask. There is a good chance we may already have something similar in the shop or coming soon.

Do you ship guitars & gear?

Yes, when shipping is marked as available for the item. Eligible items ship within the 50 US states. Orders under $75 before tax ship for a $6 flat rate, and eligible orders of $75 or more before tax ship free.

Some items remain local pickup or local delivery only because of size, weight, fragility, or other handling concerns. Each product page will indicate whether shipping is available.

What is your return policy?

Most eligible gear may be returned within 7 days, provided it comes back in the same condition. For in-store purchases, the 7-day window begins at pickup. For shipped online purchases, the 7-day window begins when the item is delivered.

Shipping and local delivery charges are not refundable, and customers are responsible for insured return shipping unless we made a mistake with the order.

In-store financing purchases are eligible for return, but approved financing returns are subject to a 6% return processing/restocking fee because Affirm/Klarna financing fees are not refunded to Coal Creek Guitars.

Some items may have additional restrictions, so please ask before purchasing if you are unsure.

Do you charge sales tax?

Yes. Coal Creek Guitars collects applicable sales tax in accordance with the laws and requirements of the State of Colorado as well as applicable local taxing authorities, including Englewood and other jurisdictions when required. Sales tax is calculated based on the nature of the transaction and the applicable local tax rates at the time of sale.

Coal Creek Guitars can help connect students with qualified guitar instructors through our local lesson network. Serial Decoders

My serial number didn't decode. Can I find out more about it?

You usually do not need to take any additional action. If a serial number fails to decode in one of our tools, the failed lookup is automatically flagged for review on our side. We then research the brand and serial number format independently to determine whether the serial number is valid and whether support can be added to the decoder.

In many cases, the serial number turns out to be legitimate — it simply uses a format that has not been added to the system yet. When that happens, we update the decoder so it works not only for you, but also for anyone else who enters a serial number in that format for that brand in the future.

We recommend waiting a few days and trying the serial number again, as decoder updates are added regularly.

Do your serial number decoders get updated?

Yes — our serial number decoders are continuously updated and improved over time. We log all serial number lookups, including both successful decodes and failed attempts. When a serial number fails to decode, it enters a review process where we independently research the brand, format, and available manufacturing information to determine whether support can be added.

In many cases, failed serial numbers are simply using formats that have not yet been added to the system. When we confirm a format is valid, we update the decoder so future searches for that brand and serial number style will return proper results. This ongoing process helps make the tools more accurate and more useful for the entire guitar community over time.

I want to decode a serial number for a brand you don't currently offer. Can you add it?

Absolutely. We are always open to expanding our serial number decoder library to support additional brands. While we currently offer decoders for 25+ major guitar and music gear brands, we understand there are many smaller, older, boutique, and less common brands that are still incredibly important to the people who own and play those instruments.

If there is a brand you would like us to support, simply reach out through our Contact Us page and let us know the brand name along with a serial number example you are trying to decode. We will review the information, research the format, and see what we can do to add support for it in the future.
