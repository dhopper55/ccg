Section 02 · The Process

How Consignment Works

Every step after intake is something we do with you, not to you. Here's exactly what happens from drop-off to payout.

01
Bring It In
Free, no-obligation evaluation. We inspect the instrument in detail — condition, originality, and anything worth noting before it ever goes up for sale.
02
Get A Written Report
You get a written condition report and a proposed sales strategy — not a verbal guess you have to take on faith.
03
Agree On Price, Together
We agree on an initial listing price and an acceptable pricing range up front, together. Any move outside that range gets run by you first, by email or text.
04
We List & Market It
Professional photos, a detailed write-up, and placement in our showroom, our online shop, and — with your approval — Reverb.com. Promoted exactly like gear we own outright.
05
Get Paid, Fast
When it sells, you get your 80% share within 14 days of payment clearing on our end — via Venmo, Zelle, or bank transfer, whichever you prefer.

How We Choose Where To List

We generally try to sell locally first — it typically offers the best profit and the best odds of a good sale. With your approval, we may also or instead list on Reverb.com, our preferred online marketplace for musical gear. Reverb's marketplace fee runs about 5–8% depending on the sale, and unlike local-sale costs, it comes off the top before the 80/20 split — it's never deducted from your share directly.

Either way, you're paid your 80% share within 14 days of the sale clearing, via Venmo, Zelle, or bank transfer.

Section 04 · A Collaborative Effort

Protection & Trust

Consignment only works if you trust who's holding your gear. Here's what's actually in writing, in plain language — and it's built so decisions are made together, not handed down.

Condition & Responsibility
We take responsibility for your instrument's condition at intake, as documented in your written evaluation report. This doesn't cover natural dry-storage cracking or pre-existing conditions we've already noted with you.
Damage, Loss, or Theft
If something is damaged, lost, or stolen while it's in our hands, we pay you 80% of its current listed price — the same standard exceptions above apply.
Pricing Is A Shared Decision
We agree on an acceptable pricing range together at intake. Any proposed change outside that range is communicated to you by email or text before it takes effect — never after.
Term & Return Of Unsold Gear
Your agreement runs until an expiration date we set together. Anything that hasn't sold by then comes back to you, no cost, unless we agree in writing to extend it.

Section 06 · Questions

Consignment FAQ

The most common questions we get from Denver-area owners looking to consign gear.

How long does the agreement last?
Until an expiration date you and we agree on when you sign. Anything that hasn't sold by then is returned to you at no cost, unless we agree in writing to extend it.
Do you sell locally, online, or both?
Both. We generally try locally first since it typically nets the best price and the best odds of a sale, and list on Reverb.com — with your approval — either instead or in addition.
Who sets the price?
You and we set the initial listing price and an acceptable pricing range together at intake. Nothing moves outside that range without you being looped in first, by email or text, before it takes effect.
What if my instrument doesn't sell?
It comes back to you at the agreement's expiration date — no cost, no obligation to extend, no questions asked.
What if something happens to my gear while it's with you?
We pay you 80% of its current listed price if it's damaged, lost, or stolen while in our possession. The usual exceptions apply for pre-existing conditions or natural dry-storage cracking already noted in your evaluation report.
How do I actually get paid?
Venmo, Zelle, or bank transfer — whichever you prefer, agreed when you sign — within 14 days of the sale clearing on our end.
