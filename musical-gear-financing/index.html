Section 01 · A Different Way To Buy

Stop Waiting For The Right Time.

If you've been eyeing something — a dream guitar, a new amp, a pedal you keep talking yourself out of — financing means you don't have to save up in a case at home while someone else buys it first. You take it home today, and pay for it on a schedule that fits your budget.

Walk Out Today

It's Layaway, Except You Don't Wait.

Old-school layaway meant paying it off before you could take it home. Affirm and Klarna flip that: you're approved, you sign, and you leave with the guitar or amp in hand — the payments happen automatically after that.

  • We process the sale like any other purchase once you're approved
  • No waiting, no holding your gear behind the counter
  • Available in-store at checkout, subject to approval
Bigger-Ticket Gear

Not Just Electrics

Financing isn't limited to new electrics and amps — it works on acoustics, used and vintage instruments, and anything else in our inventory. If it's for sale at Coal Creek Guitars, you can finance it.

Section 04 · Questions

Financing FAQ

The most common questions we hear on the sales floor.

Will checking my options hurt my credit score?
No. Both Affirm and Klarna use a soft credit check just to see what you qualify for, and that never affects your score. For some larger, longer-term Affirm plans, an additional review may happen if you choose to move forward with that specific plan — you'd always be told before that happens.
Does financing cost more than paying cash?
Purchases made with Affirm or Klarna include a 6% financing fee, added to your total, since those services charge us a fee on every financed transaction. Paying by cash, check, debit, or credit card avoids this fee entirely. Any interest on a monthly plan is separate and set by Affirm or Klarna based on your plan and credit.
Are there hidden fees?
No. Affirm never charges late fees. Klarna may charge a small fee if a payment is missed, but it's shown to you transparently before you agree to anything — never a surprise.
Do I really get to leave with the gear today?
Yes. Once you're approved, we process the sale like any other purchase, and you walk out the door with your new gear the same day.
What's the difference between Pay in 4 and monthly financing?
Pay in 4 splits smaller purchases into four interest-free payments over six weeks. Monthly financing is built for bigger-ticket gear — dream guitars, vintage pieces, full rigs — and stretches the cost over 3 to 36+ months, with interest based on your credit.
Can I finance anything in your inventory?
Yes, subject to approval — any guitar, amp, pedal, or piece of gear we sell can be financed. Financing completes in our Englewood showroom; you can browse and pick out gear online first, but Affirm and Klarna checkout isn't available for online-only purchases.
