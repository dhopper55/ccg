01 / Our Story

Where it started

Founded in 2017 by lifelong players. Englewood, Colorado.

Coal Creek Guitars was founded in 2017 by lifelong players and music enthusiasts who wanted to build something more personal than a typical gear dealer. What started as a passion project has grown into a full-service guitar shop based in Englewood, Colorado — a trusted destination for players at every level who are looking for quality instruments, honest expertise, and a place that takes the instrument seriously.

Our team has been immersed in the world of guitars for decades. Playing, collecting, restoring, and chasing tone are not things we do on the side — they are what we do. That experience shapes everything from how we evaluate a used instrument to how we talk with a first-time buyer trying to figure out what guitar is right for them.

02 / Showroom

Come play something

South Broadway corridor, Englewood. Appointment now — public hours Summer 2026.

We are located in Englewood, Colorado, just south of Denver along the South Broadway corridor. Coal Creek Guitars has a dedicated showroom where players can come in, plug into a real amp, and spend unhurried time with the gear. We keep a selection of guitars, amps, and effects on hand so you can actually dial in tone and feel — not just look at something hanging on a wall.

The showroom is currently open by appointment, with regular public hours planned for Summer 2026. If you see something you are interested in or just want to come by and look around, reach out and we will set up a time.

03 / Inventory

Buy, sell, trade

Quality used guitars, amps, pedals, and accessories. Ships within the 50 US states.

Buying, selling, and trading quality used guitars, amps, pedals, effects, and accessories is at the core of what we do. We are selective in what we acquire — every piece goes through a careful evaluation of condition, originality, playability, and value before we commit. From there, each instrument gets cleaned, detailed, and properly set up before it goes up for sale.

We ship eligible items within the 50 US states, so finding great gear at Coal Creek Guitars does not require a drive to Englewood. We also document the restoration process in free videos — from initial pickup through the final play test — so players can see exactly what went into bringing an instrument back to life.

04 / Repair Shop

Repair & setup

We diagnose before we quote. See full pricing →

Coal Creek Guitars is an active repair shop. Whether a guitar is buzzing, cutting out, or simply not playing the way it should, we can diagnose the problem and recommend the right fix before any work begins.

Setup

Playability

Full setups for electric, acoustic, and bass. Floyd Rose and locking tremolo. Seasonal neck adjustments, nut work, and action optimization.

Electronics

Diagnostics

Hum, crackle, signal loss, tuning instability. Pickup swaps, wiring work, output jack replacement, potentiometers, and shielding.

Hardware

Upgrades

Locking tuners, bridge and tailpiece replacement, strap lock installs, nut upgrades, and hardware swaps.

06 / Community

Colorado music & beyond

Colorado has one of the most vibrant music communities in the country, and the Denver south metro area is a big part of that. The South Broadway corridor in Englewood has long been a hub for independent music businesses, live venues, and the kind of eclectic creative energy that draws musicians. The Gothic Theatre down the road has hosted some of the most important artists in rock, metal, indie, and alternative music for decades — and the neighborhood around it reflects that same spirit.

We believe strongly in the power of music education. Learning to play develops creativity, discipline, and emotional expression in ways that benefit students long after the lesson ends. Coal Creek Guitars actively supports music programs in local schools because we believe every child deserves the chance to discover what music can do for them.

Military Discount — 12% Off

The owners of Coal Creek Guitars come from a proud military family, and we have an enormous amount of respect for the men and women who serve — and for the families who stand behind them. As a small way of saying thank you, we offer a 12% discount off our lowest price on eligible items for active duty, veterans, and immediate family members. That is a higher rate than most major retailers offer, and we do not cap the maximum dollar amount. Just let us know when you reach out.
