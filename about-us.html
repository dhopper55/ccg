Our Story

Coal Creek Guitars was founded in 2017 by lifelong players and music enthusiasts who wanted to build something more personal than a typical gear dealer. What started as a passion project has grown into a full-service guitar shop based in Englewood, Colorado — a trusted destination for players at every level who are looking for quality instruments, honest expertise, and a place that takes the instrument seriously.

Our team has been immersed in the world of guitars for decades. Playing, collecting, restoring, and chasing tone are not things we do on the side — they are what we do. That experience shapes everything from how we evaluate a used instrument to how we talk with a first-time buyer trying to figure out what guitar is right for them.

Our Showroom in Englewood

We are located in Englewood, Colorado, just south of Denver along the South Broadway corridor. Coal Creek Guitars now has a dedicated showroom where players can come in, plug into a real amp, and spend unhurried time with the gear. We keep a selection of guitars, amps, and effects on hand so you can actually dial in tone and feel — not just look at something hanging on a wall.

The showroom is currently open by appointment, with regular public hours planned for Summer 2026. If you see something you are interested in or just want to come by and look around, reach out and we will set up a time.

Buying, Selling & Trading

Buying, selling, and trading quality used guitars, amps, pedals, effects, and accessories is at the core of what we do. We are selective in what we acquire — every piece goes through a careful evaluation of condition, originality, playability, and value before we commit. From there, each instrument gets cleaned, detailed, and properly set up before it goes up for sale.

We ship eligible items within the 50 US states, so finding great gear at Coal Creek Guitars does not require a drive to Englewood. We also document the restoration process in free videos — from initial pickup through the final play test — so players can see exactly what went into bringing an instrument back to life.

Repair & Setup Services

Coal Creek Guitars is an active repair shop. Whether a guitar is buzzing, cutting out, or simply not playing the way it should, we can diagnose the problem and recommend the right fix before any work begins. See our full repair services and pricing for details.

Service Area

Setup & Playability

Full setups for electric, acoustic, and bass guitars. Floyd Rose and locking tremolo setups. Seasonal neck adjustments and fret buzz correction. Nut work and action optimization so every guitar plays at its best.

Service Area

Electronics & Diagnostics

Diagnostics for any playability or electronics issue — hum, crackle, signal loss, tuning instability. Pickup swaps, wiring work, output jack replacement, potentiometer repairs, and shielding. We inspect first and recommend before any work begins.

Service Area

Hardware & Upgrades

Locking tuner installs, bridge and tailpiece replacement, strap lock installs, nut upgrades, and hardware swaps. Practical upgrades that make a real difference in how a guitar plays, stays in tune, and holds up over time.

Colorado's Music Community

Colorado has one of the most vibrant music communities in the country, and the Denver south metro area is a big part of that. The South Broadway corridor in Englewood has long been a hub for independent music businesses, live venues, and the kind of eclectic creative energy that draws musicians. The Gothic Theatre down the road has hosted some of the most important artists in rock, metal, indie, and alternative music for decades — and the neighborhood around it reflects that same spirit.

We are proud to be part of this community, serving players of all genres and skill levels who share a genuine appreciation for the instrument. Whether you are a seasoned collector, a gigging musician, or someone picking up a guitar for the first time, you belong here.

Supporting Music Education

We believe strongly in the power of music education. Learning to play an instrument develops creativity, discipline, and emotional expression in ways that benefit students long after the lesson ends. Music education is not a luxury — it is an essential part of a well-rounded childhood, and far too many schools are cutting programs that deserve to thrive.

Coal Creek Guitars actively supports music programs in local schools because we believe every child deserves the chance to discover what music can do for them. When you do business with us, you are part of that too.
