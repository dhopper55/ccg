About Coal Creek Guitars

Our Story

Coal Creek Guitars was founded in 2017 by lifelong players and music enthusiasts who wanted to build something more personal than a typical gear dealer. What started as a passion project has grown into a full-service guitar shop based in Englewood, Colorado — a trusted destination for players at every level who are looking for quality instruments, honest expertise, and a place that takes the instrument seriously.

Our team has been immersed in the world of guitars for decades. Playing, collecting, restoring, and chasing tone are not things we do on the side — they are what we do. That experience shapes everything from how we evaluate a used instrument to how we talk with a first-time buyer trying to figure out what guitar is right for them.

Our Showroom in Englewood

We are located in Englewood, Colorado, just south of Denver along the South Broadway corridor. Coal Creek Guitars now has a dedicated showroom where players can come in, plug into a real amp, and spend unhurried time with the gear. We keep a selection of guitars, amps, and effects on hand so you can actually dial in tone and feel — not just look at something hanging on a wall.

The showroom is currently open by appointment, with regular public hours planned for Summer 2026. If you see something you are interested in or just want to come by and look around, reach out and we will set up a time.

Englewood and the South Broadway corridor have a long history tied to music, live performance, and the kind of independent businesses that give a neighborhood its character. The Gothic Theatre — one of Colorado's most iconic live music venues — sits just down the road from our shop. Read more about Englewood, South Broadway, and the Gothic Theatre and the history of the neighborhood we call home.

What We Do

Coal Creek Guitars buys, sells, and trades quality used guitars, amps, pedals, effects, and accessories. We are selective in what we acquire — every piece goes through a careful evaluation of condition, originality, playability, and value before we commit. From there, each instrument gets cleaned, detailed, and set up properly before it is listed for sale. We ship eligible items within the 50 US states, so you do not have to be local to find great gear here.

We also film and share the restoration process in free videos — from the initial pickup to the final play test — so players can see exactly what went into bringing an instrument back to life. We find that documentation builds trust and helps buyers understand what they are getting, and we enjoy sharing that knowledge with the wider guitar community.

Beyond buying and selling, Coal Creek Guitars is an active repair shop. Our services include full setups and playability adjustments for electric, acoustic, and bass guitars; Floyd Rose and locking tremolo setups; electronics diagnostics and repair; pickup swaps and wiring; locking tuner installs; hardware upgrades; and seasonal neck adjustments. If a guitar is not playing the way it should, we can diagnose what is happening and recommend the right fix before any work begins. See our full repair services and pricing for details.

We also maintain a local instructor network and can connect students with qualified teachers for guitar, bass, voice, and many other instruments. Teaching styles, formats, and rates vary by instructor. Contact us with your goals, experience level, and schedule and we will help find the right fit.

Free Tools & Resources

One of the things that sets Coal Creek Guitars apart is the free tools and resources we build for the guitar community. We create the same kind of tools we want when we are researching a guitar — practical, straightforward, and genuinely useful.

Our serial number decoder library covers 25+ major guitar and music gear brands, including Gibson, Fender, Ibanez, Taylor, Martin, PRS, Epiphone, Gretsch, Rickenbacker, and many more. These tools are free to use and continuously updated as we research new formats and serial number systems. If a serial number fails to decode, it enters a review process on our side and we work to add support so future lookups for that format will succeed.

We also offer a free Guitar Evaluation Report — a personalized assessment of what your instrument is worth in today's used market. Whether you are thinking about selling, trading in, or just curious about the current value of a guitar you own, it is a no-obligation way to get an honest picture before making any decisions.

For players just getting started, we have put together a collection of free practice resources covering the fundamentals that matter most in the early stages of learning.

Colorado's Music Community

Colorado has one of the most vibrant music communities in the country, and the Denver south metro area is a big part of that. The South Broadway corridor in Englewood has long been a hub for independent music businesses, live venues, and the kind of eclectic creative energy that draws musicians. The Gothic Theatre down the road from our shop has hosted some of the most important artists in rock, metal, indie, and alternative music for decades — and the neighborhood around it reflects that same spirit.

We are proud to be part of this community, serving players of all genres and skill levels who share a genuine appreciation for the instrument. Whether you are a seasoned collector, a gigging musician, or someone just picking up a guitar for the first time, you belong here.

Supporting Music Education

We believe strongly in the power of music education. Learning to play an instrument develops creativity, discipline, and emotional expression in ways that benefit students long after the lesson ends. Music education is not a luxury — it is an essential part of a well-rounded childhood, and far too many schools are cutting programs that deserve to thrive.

Coal Creek Guitars actively supports music programs in local schools because we believe every child deserves the chance to discover what music can do for them. When you do business with us, you are part of that too.

A Proud Military Family

The owners of Coal Creek Guitars come from a proud military family, and we have an enormous amount of respect for the men and women who serve — and for the families who stand behind them. As a small way of saying thank you, we offer a 12% military discount off our lowest price on eligible items. That is a higher discount than most major retailers offer, and we do not cap the maximum dollar amount. If you are active duty, a veteran, or an immediate family member of someone who served, just let us know when you reach out.
