Report sections

Every section, explained.

01
Identity

Exact model, year, factory, and run. We decode your serial number and identify the specific variant — Custom Shop vs USA, production run, and any factory options that matter to authenticity.

02
Photo Analysis

Annotated plates of your submitted photos — body, headstock, neck, hardware, electronics cavity — checked for consistency with genuine examples.

03
Full Specifications

Complete spec sheet: body wood, top, neck profile, fretboard, scale length, hardware grade, finish, electronics, and everything that ships with the instrument.

04
Authenticity Markers

Logos, fonts, hardware stamps, wiring, and construction details compared against known factory records and common counterfeit patterns.

05
Red Flags & Inconsistencies

Anything that doesn't line up — mismatched serials, wrong-era parts, incorrect hardware — called out specifically, or a clean bill if none are found.

06
Expert Verdict

A clear determination — genuine, likely genuine, inconclusive, or likely not authentic — with a confidence level and the reasoning behind it.

07
Certificate & Summary

A shareable summary of the findings and verdict — useful for a buyer, a seller, or your own records.
